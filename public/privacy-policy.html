Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  ·  Application: ASAN-Study_tool  ·  Contact: invcorporation7@gmail.com

ASAN-Study_tool (“we”, “our”, or “the Application”), created by Arham Imam, is an academic productivity and A4 study workspace. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use ASAN-Study_tool on the web or as an installed Progressive Web App (PWA).

1. Information We Collect

  • Free Tier (No Login): When you use the Free Tier without signing in, your notebooks, tasks, calendar events, custom countdowns, and timer logs are stored locally inside your browser’s localStorage and remain on your device.
  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication) or Email & Password authentication, we store your display name, email address, generated username, 5-character UID, profile photo URL (if provided by Google), plan tier, points ledger, and assigned role in our PostgreSQL database. Passwords for Email & Password accounts are salted and hashed with cryptographic scrypt and are never stored in plain text.
  • Study Workspace Content: When signed in, we store the A4 notebooks, note pages, tasks, calendar events, exam blueprints, countdown timers, study session logs, Tool Mail messages, Study Material uploads, and Contact support inquiries that you explicitly create or submit.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-ZLDZEPQJX2) to measure aggregate page views, active sessions, and app stability. We do not send note contents, passwords, or OAuth tokens to analytics.

2. Optional Google Workspace API Access & Limited Use Policy

Google Workspace integrations are strictly optional. Standard sign-in does not request Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Meet scopes. You are only asked for Google Workspace permissions when you explicitly opt in via the permission prompts or under View Plans and Settings → Google Workspace Function Permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Used solely to create and manage the dedicated ASAN-user-content folder and files created or opened by ASAN-Study_tool (PDF exports, .txt task/note backups, and study materials). The app cannot access unrelated files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents — Used when you explicitly click to export A4 study notes as Google Docs.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — Used when you explicitly sync your calendar schedule, tasks table, or master → test_dates exam master sheets with Google Sheets.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created & https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.readonly — Used only when you explicitly click to generate or view an instant Google Meet study room link.
Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use): ASAN-Study_tool’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. Google OAuth access tokens are held in memory only for your active session, are never written to localStorage or sessionStorage, and Google Workspace data is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI/ML models.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, synchronize, and persist your notes, tasks, calendars, timers, and PDF exports across devices.
  • To compute and verify study reward points, Pro plan entitlements, license key activations, and contributor royalties.
  • To deliver in-app Tool Mail, notifications, and support inquiry responses.
  • To prevent abuse, verify point transactions, and maintain service reliability and security.

4. Data Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data is processed only by core infrastructure providers required to operate the service:

  • Google / Firebase Authentication & Google APIs: Identity verification and user-authorized Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Meet actions.
  • PostgreSQL / Supabase & Application Hosting (Render / Cloud Run): Encrypted database and application runtime storage for signed-in user records.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Aggregate traffic and usage metrics.

Study Materials published publicly by approved contributors and public Tool Mail broadcasts are visible to other signed-in users as intended by those features.

5. Data Retention, Controls & Deletion

  • Granular Workspace Toggles: You can disable individual Google Workspace capabilities (Drive Sync, Docs Export, Sheets Sync, Meet Rooms) at any time in View Plans and Settings.
  • Revoke Google Access: You may revoke ASAN-Study_tool’s Google permissions at any time from your Google Account Third-Party Access Settings .
  • Delete Your Data: You can delete individual notebooks, tasks, calendar events, countdowns, messages, and uploaded materials inside the app, clear local Free Tier storage in your browser settings, or request full account deletion by contacting invcorporation7@gmail.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

ASAN-Study_tool is built for students preparing for board and competitive examinations. Users under the minimum digital consent age in their jurisdiction should use the Free Tier locally or obtain parent/guardian permission before creating an account or linking a Google account.

7. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Material changes will update the Effective Date above. If you have questions or privacy requests, please contact Arham Imam at invcorporation7@gmail.com or via the in-app Contact page.
